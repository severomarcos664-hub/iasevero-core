IASevero v123.5 — Decision Provenance Foundation

MISSION
Define how runtime decisions are traced, explained and connected to the CanonicalOperationalModel.

DEFINITION
DecisionProvenance records who decided, why the decision was made,
which policy participated, and how the decision affected execution.

CORE STRUCTURE

DecisionProvenance
├── provenanceId
├── sessionId
├── requestId
├── traceId
├── correlationId
├── source
│   ├── decisionGate
│   ├── actionPolicy
│   ├── executiveAuthority
│   ├── providerGovernor
│   └── runtimeEvaluation
├── decision
│   ├── executionAllowed
│   ├── providerAllowed
│   ├── runtimeAction
│   └── providerSelected
├── rationale
│   ├── decisionReason
│   ├── policyApplied
│   ├── blockingReason
│   └── confidence
├── operationalState
└── version

PURPOSE
Enable explanation, replay, audit, dashboard visibility and runtime evaluation.

RULE
Decision provenance must derive from existing runtime authority, policy,
provider and execution signals. Do not create parallel decision authority.

STATUS
Decision Provenance Foundation prepared for validation.
